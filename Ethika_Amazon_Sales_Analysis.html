Units Sold vs ASP

Volume collapsed as prices rose 32%

Finding #1 — ASP increase drove the entire gap. 2024 ASP $16.09 vs 2025 $21.29 (+32%). Units dropped 69% (657k→207k). Revenue fell 58% ($10.6M→$4.4M). Customers are highly price-elastic for basic staples.
Finding #2 — December 2024 alone = $2.04M (114k units). The Q4 holiday push accounted for 19% of full-year 2024 revenue. 2025 December was only $473k (−77%). Aggressive holiday pricing was the single biggest driver.
Finding #3 — Boys division collapsed 78%. Boys: $3.78M (2024) → $820k (2025). Boys Carryover specifically dropped 82%. Mens fell 47% ($6.55M→$3.44M).
Finding #4 — 2025 ran zero promotions for 4 months straight (May–Aug). 2024 ran $8–13k/month every single month. This absence directly suppressed unit volume in H2 2025.
